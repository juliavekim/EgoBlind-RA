# Data provenance and terms

## What is in this repository

| Path | Contents | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| `data/train_labeled.csv`, `data/test_labeled.csv` | EgoBlind question splits annotated with our binary `urgency` label | Question/answer fields from EgoBlind; the `urgency` column is ours |
| `data/baseline_frames/` | 20,098 JPEG frames sampled around each question timestamp | Decoded from EgoBlind source videos |
| `results/` | Model predictions, scores, and analysis outputs | Generated by this project |

## EgoBlind terms

The videos and question annotations come from the **EgoBlind** benchmark:

> Xiao et al., *EgoBlind: Towards Egocentric Visual Assistance for the Blind*, 2025.
> [arXiv:2503.08221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08221) · <https://github.com/doc-doc/EgoBlind>

EgoBlind states that the dataset **may be used for research purposes only**, and that
redistribution must **attach the source links of the videos**. The frames in
`data/baseline_frames/` are derived images, redistributed here for research
reproducibility only. Per-video source links are maintained by the EgoBlind authors in
the video-source list published in their repository; consult that list for the
provenance of any individual `video_name`.

If you are the EgoBlind maintainer or a source-video rights holder and want any derived
frames removed, please open an issue and we will take them down.

## Privacy note

EgoBlind videos are first-person recordings from the daily lives of blind and visually
impaired individuals. Frames may incidentally contain identifiable people, locations,
documents, or other personal information belonging to recorders and to bystanders who
did not consent to redistribution. Treat this data accordingly: use it for research,
do not attempt re-identification, and do not redistribute it further.
